
MASONBRO COMPANY INFORMATION

Company:
MasonBro is a home masonry and construction services company.

Location:
MasonBro currently serves customers in Bangalore, India.

Ownership:
MasonBro is co-founded by Arpit Lokwani and N Sushmi.
Arpit Lokwani is the CTO.
N Sushmi is the Product Lead and Founder.

Tagline:
Your Trusted Mason, Just a Tap Away.

SERVICES:

1. Brickwork and Wall Construction
MasonBro provides brickwork and wall construction services
for residential construction requirements.

2. Plastering
MasonBro provides plastering services for residential properties.

3. Flooring and Tiling
MasonBro provides flooring and tile installation services.

BOOKING PROCESS:

1. Customers select the service they require.
2. Customers submit a booking request through MasonBro.
3. The MasonBro operations team assigns a suitable mason.
4. The assigned mason contacts the customer through phone
   or WhatsApp.
5. The mason may request photos and additional details.
6. A site inspection may be arranged when required.
7. The mason provides a quotation.
8. Work begins after the quotation is approved.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT:

Customers can submit booking requests through the MasonBro
website or mobile application.

The operations team coordinates mason assignments.

PRICING:

Pricing depends on the type of work, project size,
materials, site conditions and labour requirements.

Customers should request an assessment and quotation
for accurate pricing.

SERVICE AREA:

MasonBro currently operates in Bangalore, India.

If a customer asks about service availability outside
Bangalore, explain that availability needs confirmation.

IMPORTANT:

Never invent prices, service availability, guarantees,
or booking confirmations.

If information is not available in the knowledge base,
tell the customer that the MasonBro team needs to confirm it.
